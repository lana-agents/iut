## 1. Exact statements (T3 line numbers checked 2026-10-18)

* **Prop 1.1 (l.1639–1657), linear systems.** Let `X` be proper and `k` algebraically closed, and
  set `Γ^×(E) = {f ∈ K_X | Div(f) + E ≥ 0}`, `l(E) = dim_k Γ(X, O_X(E))`. Then:
  * (i) `Γ^×(D) ≅ Γ(X, O_X(D)) \ {0}`, compatibly with the free `k^×`-actions;
  * (ii) "`l(D)` is equal to the smallest nonnegative integer `d` such that there exists an
    effective divisor `E` of degree `d` on `X` for which `Γ^×(D − E) = ∅`".
* **Prop 1.2 (l.1658–1690), additive structure via linear systems.**
  * (i) There are distinct points `x, y₁, y₂ ∉ Supp(D)` with `l(D) = 2` and `l(D − e₁ − e₂) = 0`
    for all `e₁ ≠ e₂` in `{x, y₁, y₂}`.
  * (ii) For `λ ∈ k^×` there is a unique `f_{λ,i} ∈ Γ^×(D)` with `f(x) = λ`, `f(y_i) ≠ 0` and
    `f(y_{3−i}) = 0`.
  * (iii) If `λ/μ ≠ −1`, then `f_{λ,1} + f_{μ,2}` is "the unique element `g ∈ Γ^×(D)` such that
    `g(y₁) = f_{λ,1}(y₁)`, `g(y₂) = f_{μ,2}(y₂)`", and `λ + μ = g(x)`.
  * Proof (l.1680–1690): start from any `D` with `l(D) ≥ 2`, subtract points until `l(D) = 2`, then
    choose `x`, `y₁`, `y₂` where suitable sections do not vanish.
* **Prop 1.3 (l.1691–1743), Uchida's lemma** ("implicit in the argument of [Uchi], §3, Lemmas
  8-11 [cf. also [Tama], Lemma 4.7]"). "a functorial algorithm for constructing the additive
  structure on `K_X^× ∪ {0}` […] from the following data:
  * (a) the [abstract!] group `K_X^×`;
  * (b) the set of [surjective] homomorphisms `V_X = {ord_x : K_X^× ↠ ℤ}_{x∈X(k)}` […];
  * (c) for each `v = ord_x ∈ V_X`, the subgroup `U_v ⊆ K_X^×` given by the `f` such that
    `f(x) = 1`."

  Functoriality is "with respect to isomorphisms […] of such triples". Proof (l.1722–1743):
  * `k^× = ⋂_v Ker(v)`;
  * `Ker(v) = U_v × k^×` gives the evaluation at `v`;
  * `V_X` gives divisors, `Γ^×(D)` and `l(D)` (via Prop 1.1(ii));
  * then Prop 1.2(ii), (iii) give `+` on `k^×`, and evaluation gives `+` on `K_X^×`.
* **Prop 1.4 (l.1752–1806), synchronization of geometric cyclotomes.** `X` is proper and
  `char k = 0`.
  * (i) `I_x ≅ Ẑ(1)`, and `Ker(Δ_U ↠ Δ_{U_x})` is normally generated by the cusp inertia groups.
  * (ii) There is an exact sequence `1 → I_x → Δ^{c-cn}_{U_x} → Δ_X → 1`, where `c-cn` is the
    maximal cuspidally central quotient ([Cusp] Def 1.1(i)). The transgression of
    `1 ∈ Hom(I_x, I_x)` gives the natural isomorphism `M_X = Hom(H²(Δ_X, Ẑ), Ẑ) ≅ I_x`.
  * Proof: (ii) is "[Mzk19], Proposition 1.6, (iii)".
* **Def 1.5 (l.1807–1836), Kummer-faithful.** For every finite `k_H/k` and every semi-abelian
  variety (respectively torus) `A/k_H`, `⋂_N N·A(k_H) = 0`, or equivalently the Kummer map is
  injective. Rem 1.5.4(i) (l.1910): sub-p-adic fields are Kummer-faithful.
* **Prop 1.6 (l.1969–2039), Kummer classes of rational functions** (`k` Kummer-faithful).
  * (i) `κ_U : Γ(U, O^×) → H¹(Π_U, M_X)` is injective.
  * (ii) `t_D : G_k → Π_{J^d}`; for `d = 0`, `t_D` is the identity section iff `D` is principal.
  * (iii) For `U = X \ S`, `S ⊆ X(k)` finite, there is an exact sequence
    `1 → (k^×)^∧ → H¹(Π_U, M_X) → ⊕_{x∈S} Ẑ`, and the image of `Γ(U, O^×)` modulo `(k^×)^∧` is
    the inverse image of the principal divisors supported in `S`.
* **Def 1.7 (l.2040–2050).** NF-curves, NF-points, NF-rational functions, NF-constants.
* **Prop 1.8 (l.2061–2106).** NF-functions and NF-constants are characterised inside
  `P_U ⊆ H¹(Π_U, M_X)` by their restrictions `s_x^*` to decomposition groups of NF-points.
* **Thm 1.9 (l.2109–2199).** `X` of strictly Belyi type ([AbsTopII] Def 3.5, T2 l.4614: "defined over
  a number field and isogenous […] to a hyperbolic curve of genus zero") over a sub-p-adic
  field ([LocAn] Def 15.4(i), LP l.4301). There is "a functorial "group-theoretic" algorithm […] for
  reconstructing the "NF-portion of the function field" of `X` from […]
  `1 → Δ_X → Π_X → G_k → 1`". The steps:
  * (a) l.2131: `Π_U ↠ Π_Y` and the decomposition groups of NF-points, by Belyi cuspidalization
    ([AbsTopII] Cor 3.7(a)–(c), T2 l.4698–4775);
  * (b) l.2144: `I_z ≅ μ_Ẑ(Π_U) = M_Z` via Prop 1.4(ii) (genus `≥ 2`, Rem 1.10.1(ii));
  * (c) l.2153: `P_U ⊆ H¹(Π_U, μ_Ẑ(Π_U))` via Prop 1.6(ii), (iii);
  * (d) l.2158: `k̄_NF^× ⊆ K^×_{Z_NF} ↪ lim→_V H¹(Π_V, μ_Ẑ(Π_U))` via Prop 1.8;
  * (e) l.2179: the additive structure via Prop 1.3.

  Functoriality (l.2193): w.r.t. open injective homomorphisms and base change. Proof (l.2198):
  "immediate from the various results cited".
* **Cor 1.10 (l.2352–2543), MLF case.**
  * (a) l.2375: `μ_{ℚ/ℤ}(G_k) = lim→_H (H^ab)_tors` along the transfer, and
    `H²(G_k, μ_Ẑ(G_k)) ≅ Ẑ`.
  * (b) l.2401: `H¹(G_k, μ_Ẑ(G_k)) ≅ G_k^ab ↠ Ẑ` (Frobenius).
  * (c) l.2423: `μ_Ẑ(G_k) ≅ μ_Ẑ(Π_X)`, using the positive rational structure of [AbsAnab] Lemma
    2.5 (weights).
  * (d) l.2434: the image of `k^×` in `H¹(G_k, μ_Ẑ(Π_X))` as the inverse image of `ℤ·Frob`.
  * (d′) l.2449: a construction independent of (c), via Thm 1.9(e).
  * (e)–(h): the function field `K_X` itself, using [GalSect] Lemma 3.1 and [Cusp] Thm 2.1.
  * Rem 1.10.1 (l.2544): normalisation by the index for open subgroups.
  * Rem 1.10.3(ii) (l.2572): the κ-cyclotome `μ^κ_Ẑ(Π_X) = Hom(ℚ/ℤ, κ(k̄_NF^×))`, which avoids
    (c).
